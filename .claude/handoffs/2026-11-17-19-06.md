## Goal
Automated GTA 6 clip production. The pipeline watches live streams and videos, finds the best moments, cuts them into vertical captioned clips, and posts them to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. The aim is maximum views on the user's own channels. GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time, which is New Zealand Wed Nov 18 11:00 UTC, AU east 13:00 UTC, Central Europe 23:00 UTC, UK Thu 00:00 UTC and US Thu 05:00 UTC. Live mode must be running on NZ/AU streams by Wed 11:00 UTC. Being first matters, but quality comes first.

## Decisions and dead ends
- **Anonymous IRC with raw sockets:** no new dependency (no websockets).
- **Tier B rewind:** Twitch exposes only about 30–50 s of backlog (measured), so the fetch must start at the spike, not after the post-roll. It records forward to 80 s total.
- **Tier A chunks:** a single growing file drifts from wall-clock time after Twitch ad gaps. ffmpeg `-segment_time` with `-strftime 1` and `%s` names chunks by epoch (verified on macOS).
- **Duration check:** `ffprobe` duration on a raw `.ts` is wrong (it reported 1416 s for a 20 s file). Tier B is remuxed to mp4 with `-avoid_negative_ts make_zero` before measuring.
- **YAML gotcha:** `no` parses as boolean False, so it is quoted in `hype_words`. The code casts words to `str`.
- **Baseline bug fixed:** the median baseline must only use bins from `first_idx` onward, otherwise early zero bins make everything spike.
- **Reference screenshots:** they sit in the macOS-protected `~/Library/Metadata/CoreSpotlight/PasteboardHistory`. The Read tool can't open `.heic`, and the shell and `sips` can't read that folder, even with the sandbox off. They arrived inline later. Don't retry the file paths.
- **Shell blocks:** a command with `rm` plus a relative glob after `cd` was blocked by a safety check. Foreground `sleep` is blocked, so use Monitor with an until-loop instead.
