## What the hits have in common (transcripts of the top 45)

1. **"The game is like real life" discoveries are the #1 kind of hit.** The streamer learns that GTA 6 does something real life does, and reacts with disbelief or anger:
   - "You can't steal this car? You need a key?" (7.4M)
   - The club checks your age and refuses you (2.6M, 1.2M, 1.0M and 837K: **four accounts posted the same moment and all got hits**)
   - The plane crash from real-life Miami is recreated (1.1M)
   - "hella traffic" (911K)
   - NPCs hear him through his mic (500K)
   - You can pump gas (1.1M)
   - The grocery bag disappears (588K)
2. **Lucia and relationship moments, in gameplay rather than cutscenes:**
   - Lacy doing Lucia's errands (3.9M)
   - "That's OUR girl" (2.8M)
   - Speed picking Lucia's hairstyle with chat (1.3M, 111 s)
   - Speed meeting Lucia (688K)
   - Lacy missing Lucia (963K)
3. **Short shock reactions** to something absurd: the Hulk (3.0M), "it's a Muslim! I'm so sorry, habibi" (1.9M), a parachute survival "I SURVIVED" (1.2M), a roadkill chameleon (1.4M). These run 9–16 s, with speech starting at 0.0 s.
4. **A verdict or complaint told to chat:** "only thing I don't like…" (650K), "this game shits on GTA 5" (666K), and **ohnePixel's "GTA VI on 30 FPS" (551K, his best on TikTok)**.
5. **A small win the streamer is excited about:** "Wait, she just sent my name… I'm in the game" (1.9M), "We going to the city!" (1.3M), "I'm done!" first to finish (2.3M).

**Titles:** most clipper hits use one formula: **"<Streamer> was SHOCKED / EXCITED / DISAPPOINTED / in DISBELIEF after finding out <exact GTA 6 thing>"**. Capitals go on the emotion word and the key noun. The specific thing carries the title; a vague "reacts" doesn't. (Study 1 said generic "shocked" titles sit lower. The difference is that these name the exact mechanic.)

**Length:** across clipper accounts, length doesn't predict views.

| Length | Median views |
|---|---|
| 0–10 s | 6.5K |
| 10–15 s | 8K |
| 15–20 s | 6K |
| 20–30 s | 8K |
| 30–45 s | 14K |

The few clips over 90 s did well (8 clips, median 130K): Speed's Lucia hairstyle, and ohnePixel stealing a plane at 177 s (492K). **A mini-story with a goal ("free the bear", "steal a plane") can hold 60–180 s. A single reaction should be 8–20 s.**

**Cuts:** speech starts in the first second on almost every hit. Many end within 0.5 s of the last word.

## Changes made from this

- **Judge prompt:**
  - "real-life realism" discoveries and in-gameplay Lucia/relationship moments are named as top keeps;
  - mini-story arcs are allowed to run long;
  - the title formula is "<Streamer> was SHOCKED after finding out <exact thing>".
- **A per-streamer length cap** in `streamers.yaml`: ohnePixel `max_len_s: 120` for goal-driven arcs. Everyone else stays at 60 s.
- **Re-clipping proven moments is worth it:** the same N3on club moment hit on 4 accounts. Late is fine if the moment is proven.
